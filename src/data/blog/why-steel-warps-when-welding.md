Steel expands when it gets hot and contracts as it cools.

During welding, this heating and cooling happens in a very concentrated area.

That is why fabricated parts can move, twist or bow.

## Heat Is Not Distributed Evenly

The weld zone becomes much hotter than the surrounding material.

As the weld cools, it contracts and pulls on the rest of the part.

## Welding Sequence Matters

Changing the order in which welds are made can help distribute heat more evenly.

## Fixtures Can Help

Clamps and fixtures can help maintain alignment while welding.

However, they should be used together with good heat control and welding sequence.
